Creation disclosure
Hector wrote and illustrated the story. He developed its ideas in conversation with Alicia, his personal AI agent (https://www.myalicia.com). ChatGPT and Codex supported construction of the website, and Gemini generated the generic fictional voices for the online edition. Ambient scene effects are generated by the audio-production code.

The title, chapter lettering, and illustrations are drawn in the creator’s hand, with digital tracing and preparation for print and the online reader. None of the Gemini voices was intended to imitate a real identifiable person.

Typography and quotation
The print edition embeds Libre Caslon Text, released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. It includes one attributed epigraph: “Maturity means finding again the seriousness we had as children at play.” — Friedrich Nietzsche, Beyond Good and Evil, §94.

Narration and music
The online narration was generated through the Gemini API. Local production records identify Gemini 2.5 Flash Preview TTS and the synthetic voices Sulafat, Puck, Enceladus, Algenib, Callirrhoe, Leda, and Charon.

The current English and Spanish online editions use the original narration performances and generated ambient scene effects with a new owner-selected music soundtrack. Spoken chapter and word timing is unchanged. A joyful musical coda follows the last words. Full durations: English 20:36; Spanish 20:46.
