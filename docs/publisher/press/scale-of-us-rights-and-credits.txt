Creation disclosure
Hector wrote and illustrated the story. He developed its ideas in conversation with Alicia, his personal AI agent (https://www.myalicia.com). ChatGPT and Codex supported construction of the website, and Gemini generated the generic fictional voices and sound effects for the online edition.

The title, chapter lettering, and illustrations are drawn in the creator’s hand, with digital tracing and preparation for print and the online reader. None of the Gemini voices or sound effects was intended to imitate a real identifiable person.

Typography and quotation
The print edition embeds Libre Caslon Text, released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. It includes one attributed epigraph: “Maturity means finding again the seriousness we had as children at play.” — Friedrich Nietzsche, Beyond Good and Evil, §94.

Narration and music
The online narration was generated through the Gemini API. Local production records identify Gemini 2.5 Flash Preview TTS and the synthetic voices Sulafat, Puck, Enceladus, Algenib, Callirrhoe, Leda, and Charon.

The score uses “Never Say No” by Clarence Reed, “The Adventure Begins” by Philip Ayers, “Superior” by Silver Maple, and “Happy Children” by Marc Torch. All four are Epidemic Sound catalogue tracks. They are not open-source music. Music clearance remains pending written confirmation from Epidemic Sound and is not represented as complete.
