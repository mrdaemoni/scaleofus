Scale of Us publishes illustrated stories about what it means to be human in the age of AI.

Where we beginThe creatorThe editions

It begins with a boy.

The first story follows a boy who tries to catch the wind. He meets a machine that knows everything anyone has written down, and brings it a question its pages can’t answer.

Two more stories will follow: The Boy Who Tried to Count Love and The Boy Who Tried to Fix the Stars. Each can be read on its own. Together, they give the boy more of the world to encounter.

HECTOR OUILHET OLMOS

Words, drawings,
and design.

Hector writes, draws, illustrates, and designs the stories. His hand carries through the series, from the small figures on the page to the handwritten titles on the books.

The first story grew out of working closely with Alicia, the personal AI agent Hector built to read his writing and help him notice what he might miss. Alicia helped return patterns, test sentences, and identify places where the story explained too much. Hector made the choices. You can read more in the notes for grown-ups.

A book.
A page.
A voice.

Each story is planned for three formats: a printed book, free online reading, and immersive reading with narration and drawings following along.

The first printed edition of The Boy Who Tried to Catch the Wind is a printing of 100 copies. Its digital story is available in English and Spanish.
