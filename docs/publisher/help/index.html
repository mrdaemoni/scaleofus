Reading & listening

Online reading is free. Choose English or Spanish and move at your own pace. For immersive reading, open the reader and press Listen. You can pause and choose a chapter.

If audio doesn’t start, press Listen again after checking your device’s sound settings. The text remains available while playback is paused.

Shipping

The signed printed edition ships within the United States. Shipping is $12 per order for one or two copies. Applicable sales tax is calculated at checkout. Orders normally dispatch within five business days, with tracking sent after the parcel leaves.

If an order cannot dispatch within the promised time, we will offer a revised shipping date. You may accept the delay or cancel the unshipped order for a full refund.

Returns, damage & incorrect orders

Because these are signed books from a small batch, sales are final for change of mind. Please report a damaged, defective, or incorrect book within 14 calendar days of delivery and include the order number and clear photographs.

We will replace the book when a copy is available. If replacement is unavailable, we will refund the book and its original shipping charge. Please wait for return instructions before sending anything back.

Reviews & privacy

A reviewer chooses their public name and the experience they’re reviewing. Contact details are private. Reviews from complimentary-copy recipients and readers who know the author carry those disclosures.

This local preview saves only a test review draft in your browser. It discards the email field and sends nothing to a server. You can delete the draft from the review form.

Before the live review form opens, we’ll publish the privacy notice, explain review moderation, and connect email verification and storage. Positive and critical reviews will follow the same moderation rules.
