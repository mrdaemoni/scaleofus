Creation and rights.

Written, illustrated, and designed by Hector Ouilhet Olmos. The title, chapter lettering, and illustrations are drawn in the creator’s hand, with digital preparation for print and the online reader. The Spanish-language version is by the author.

Hector wrote and illustrated the story. He developed its ideas in conversation with Alicia, his personal AI agent ↗. ChatGPT and Codex supported construction of the website, and Gemini generated the generic fictional voices for the online edition. Ambient scene effects are generated by the audio-production code.

Text and illustrations © 2026 Hector Ouilhet Olmos. All rights reserved.

Serious enquiries are welcome for translation, audiobook, film or animation, educational, library, and foreign-edition rights. All rights remain with Hector Ouilhet Olmos unless he approves a specific written agreement.

The current English and Spanish online editions use synthetic fictional voices and generated ambient scene effects, without a music soundtrack. The voices are not intended to imitate a real person. No third-party award, review, or accessibility certification is claimed.
