ABOUT SCALE OF US

Being human
in the age of AI.

We’re learning to live with AI. We need stories that help us find our bearings: who we are, what matters to us, and how we live together.

Scale of Us is a publishing house built around those questions. We bring ideas from thinkers and philosophers across time into illustrated stories anyone can read.

You can follow a boy trying to catch the wind, or sit with his question to a machine: Who am I? We hope each reading gives you something new to notice in your own life.

A little world.
Room to grow.
